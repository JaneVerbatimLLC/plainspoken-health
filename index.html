Money, voting, and health care, explained plainly.

We teach free workshops in Flatbush, Brooklyn on budgeting, credit, civic participation, and finding your way through health services. No jargon, no judgment, and nobody has to share their personal business.

Bring a workshop to your group See what we teach

What we teach

Every workshop is free for the people who attend. Participants follow a fictional character through real-life situations, so everyone learns without having to talk about their own finances or health.

Budgeting

Build a spending plan that fits your real income, from a first budget to a full intensive series.

Credit

What a credit score is, what moves it, and how to protect it from high-cost debt.

Voting

How local government shapes daily life, and how to register and vote. Strictly non-partisan.

Medication & Money

How to find programs that help with the cost and access of care. This is education, not medical advice.

Host us at your library, center, or congregation

Partner organizations provide the room and gather the group. We bring the curriculum, worksheets, and facilitation. Hosting is offered at a flat community rate, and the people you serve always attend free.

Materials are available in English, Spanish, and Haitian Creole, in large print, and with ASL interpretation on request.
